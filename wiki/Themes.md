The app comes in **Light** and **Dark**, plus five built-in themes, and you can make your own. A theme changes the colours, and can also change the corners and the font.

**Picking one.** In **Options - Appearance**, open the dropdown and pick:

- **Follow system** - Light or Dark to match Windows, and it switches when you switch Windows.
- **Light** or **Dark** - the stock look, whatever Windows does.
- **Tarkov** - near-black with warm khaki text and a brass accent.
- **OLED black** - true black behind everything, for OLED screens.
- **High contrast** - black, white and a yellow accent, with stronger borders.
- **Soft** - a softer dark grey with gentler text and a muted blue accent.
- **Steam** - the dark blue of the Steam store.
- **Your own themes** - listed under these once you've saved one.

The change happens straight away, with no restart. The built-in themes and your own stay as they are when Windows switches between light and dark. The Server page's log keeps its own colours whatever the theme.

## Making your own
Click **Customise** beside the dropdown.

- **Start from** - Dark or Light, a built-in theme, or one of yours. Starting from a built-in theme makes a copy, and the original stays as it is.
- **Name** - what the dropdown shows.
- **Base** - Light or Dark. This decides every colour you don't set.
- **Colours** - accent, surfaces (window, page, cards, inner cards, menus and tooltips, borders, dividers), text, status colours and the Mod footprint badges. Click a swatch to pick from a palette, or type a hex code: `#RRGGBB`, or `#AARRGGBB` to make it see-through. The reset button beside a row goes back to the base colour. Leave the accent empty to keep the Windows accent.
- **Corners** - Default, Square or Round.
- **Font** - any font installed on your PC.

The whole app shows each change as you make it. **Save** keeps it and switches to it. **Revert** throws away what you've changed since the last save. Closing without saving asks first, and puts back the theme you had before. **Delete** removes one of your own themes; the built-in themes can't be deleted.

**Hard-to-read text.** If a text colour is hard to read on the window, the page, cards or menus, a line under the colours says which, how far off it is and what to aim for. It's a warning only, so you can still save.

## Sharing a theme
**Export** saves the theme as a `.tcftheme` file. To use one you've been sent, open **Customise** and click **Import**. The theme is added to your list, and importing the same theme again updates it. A theme that names a font you don't have uses the default font until you pick another.

Your themes are kept in `Data\themes` inside the app's folder, one file each (see [Files and logs](Files-and-logs)).
